# Space-Runner-3D
Space Runner 3D
A fast-paced, visually stunning 3D space-themed runner game built with Three.js.
Game Overview
Space Runner 3D challenges players to navigate through an endless cosmic track while avoiding obstacles, destroying aliens, and collecting power-ups. The game features stunning 3D visuals with cosmic backgrounds, dynamic lighting, and realistic physics.
Features:
1.Immersive 3D Environment: Navigate through a space track with realistic physics and dynamic camera movement
2.Multiple Enemy Types: Dodge obstacles and shoot aliens to increase your score
3.Massive Asteroids: Large space rocks that require multiple hits to destroy and yield big point rewards
4.Power-up System: Collect various power-ups with unique effects:
 Shield: Protects from damage
 Rapid Fire: Shoots multiple lasers
 Speed Boost: Increases speed and score multiplier
5.Health System: Restore health by collecting heart-shaped health pickups
6.Combo System: Chain alien kills to increase your score multiplier
7.High Score Tracking: Beat your high score to trigger a celebration with confetti and special effects
8.Responsive Controls: Keyboard and on-screen controls for all devices
How to Play:
1.Movement: Use A/D or ←/→ arrow keys to move left/right
2.Jump: Press W or ↑ arrow key to jump over obstacles
3.Back: Press S or ↓ arrow key to move backward slightly
4.Shoot: Press Space to fire lasers at enemies
Technical Details:
1.Built with vanilla JavaScript and Three.js
2.Uses local storage to persist high scores between sessions
3.Implements advanced 3D rendering techniques including:
   Dynamic shadows and lighting
   Camera perspective effects
   Particle systems for explosions and effects
   Custom 3D models and animations
Tips for Players:
1.Prioritize collecting power-ups, especially shields before asteroids approach
2.Build up combos by continuously shooting aliens to maximize your score
3.Watch for the "ASTEROID INCOMING!" warning and prepare to dodge or destroy it
4.Collect health pickups when your health is low to stay in the game longer
5.Use the backward motion (S key) to give yourself more time to react to obstacles
About:
Space Runner 3D was created for a hackathon project to demonstrate advanced browser-based 3D gaming capabilities using modern web technologies. The entire game is contained in a single HTML file with embedded JavaScript, making it easy to run in any modern web browser without installation.
